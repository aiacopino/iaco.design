Original Works

Taking the time to appreciate the small hidden wonders of nature has always brought me a sense of peace and gratefulness for the beauty around us. My work grows out of that: Close observation of structures the world offers abundantly, composed in symmetrical and sometimes unexpected ways. What I hope to pass on, along with each piece, is a little of that same stillness. A reminder to slow down and notice.
