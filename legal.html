Privacy Policy

1. General Handling of Data

This website operates as a purely static digital portfolio. We respect your privacy and do not use invasive tracking, marketing cookies, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).

2. Contacting the Studio

We do not use a server-side contact form. When you click an inquiry button, your personal email client opens. Any personal data you choose to send (like your name or email address) is solely processed via standard email infrastructure to answer your request. It is not stored in a web database or shared with third parties.

3. Hosting Infrastructure

This website is hosted securely on GitHub Pages (Microsoft). For the purpose of delivering the site to your browser and maintaining network security, GitHub's servers automatically log technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, and timestamp. This data is handled in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.
